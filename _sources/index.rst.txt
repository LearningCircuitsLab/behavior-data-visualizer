Behavior Explorer
=================

A dashboard to look at the behavior of the mice of the Training Village, day by day.
It runs all the time on the cluster, for everyone.

.. important::

   **To use it from your computer:** :doc:`connect` (add an ``explorer`` entry
   to your SSH configuration once; then run ``ssh -N explorer`` and open
   http://localhost:8055).
