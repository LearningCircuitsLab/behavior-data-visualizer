API#

data#

Reading the data of the Training Village projects for the dashboard.

The data folder has one folder per project, with the trials of each subject in <project>/sessions/<subject>/<subject>.csv (or .csv.gz), the logs of each setup in <project>/<setup>/ and the videos in <project>/videos/<subject>/. It is read with lecilab_behavior_analysis, as the daily reports (village-report-generator) do.

The trials of the last subjects opened are kept in memory, shared by everyone using the dashboard (up to CACHE_GB, see cache.py), and read again when their file changes.

behavior_data_visualizer.figures.psychometric_trials(df, modality)[source]#

Choices on the trials of one modality, as in the daily report (see lecilab_behavior_analysis.figures.panels.psychometric):

  • visual: by brightness ratio (+ left brighter),

  • auditory-cot: by evidence strength (6 bins),

  • auditory-local-wn: by sound level (+ left), 3 bins per side,

  • otherwise, or with only easy trials: by side and difficulty.

Returns (one row per trial, with x (the mean of its bin when binned) and choice (1 left, 0 right); x axis title; whether a logistic curve can be fitted). The trials are those of the modality that are not habituation and have a first choice.

Return type:

Tuple[DataFrame, str, bool]

What happened on each trial of a day, as a barcode (one tick per trial):

main#

Training Village Behavior Explorer: a dash app to look at the behavior of the mice of a project, day by day.

Choose a project and a mouse; the calendar shows its days with trials. Click a day to see its summary, its performance, its psychometric curves (one per stimulus modality) and what happened on each trial (stimulus, outcome, early pokeouts). Click a trial in the performance or in the trial-by-trial figure to watch the video from that trial. Zoom on some trials in either figure to see the same trials in the other one, and the psychometric curves of those trials only. The buttons draw the figures of the daily report: the session summary of the day and the training history up to it.

Run it with: behavior-explorer (or python behavior_data_visualizer/main.py), then open http://127.0.0.1:8050. One dashboard can be used by several people at the same time; http://127.0.0.1:8050/status shows its version and the data in memory. See behavior-explorer –help for the options. The data is read from the folder of lecilab_behavior_analysis (the LECILAB_DATA_PATH environment variable, or the folder of this computer).
