Behavior Explorer#

A dashboard to look at the behavior of the mice of the Training Village, day by day. It runs all the time on the cluster, for everyone.

Important

To use it from your computer: Use it from your computer (add an explorer entry to your SSH configuration once; then run ssh -N explorer and open http://localhost:8055).
